### 2. What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

| What | Why | Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email address, the role you applied for, your one-time-code checks | To invite you, confirm it is you at the door, and run your assessment | Steps at your request before a possible employment contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Screen, webcam and microphone recordings, the room scan, keystroke timing, browser activity, IP address, device details | To check that the test was taken fairly by the right person | Our legitimate interest in a fair, secure assessment, Art. 6(1)(f) (you can object, section 7) |
| Your code and answers, your score, flags raised by the software, the reviewer's notes and decision, appeals | To score the test, review it and make a hiring decision | Legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f), and steps before a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Your government ID image, your selfie, face measurements used in memory to compare faces, and face measurements used during the test to check you are present and looking at the screen | To confirm your identity and the fairness of the test | **Your explicit consent**, Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a), given by a separate tick box. You may refuse or withdraw. A video call replaces the face match |

We do not collect your data from anyone else, except that the hiring team may give us your name, email address and the role. We do not use your data to train AI models. No software here tries to read your emotions. We never sell your biometric data.

### 3. Automated tools, scores and who decides

Software raises **flags** during the test (for example, a second face on camera, a large paste, or code very similar to other submissions). It also gives your code a score by running test cases, and it combines the flags into a risk score that helps a reviewer see where to look first. **A trained reviewer reviews every assessment, and a person makes every hiring decision. Nothing is rejected automatically.** Your results are not used in hiring until the reviewer has signed off. You can appeal a violation finding within 7 days to a different reviewer. This is why we believe the rules on decisions made only by automated means (GDPR Art. 22) do not apply to you; if you think a decision was made only by software, tell us.

### 4. Who receives your data

- Our staff by role: recruiters (status, scores, decision), reviewers (recordings, images, flags, code), super admins (system, your requests). Every opening of your recordings or images is logged.
- Our service providers, under contract, on our instructions only: **Amazon Web Services** (hosting, storage, email sending through Amazon SES, and logs in Amazon CloudWatch; this includes storing your biometric data) and **Cloudflare** (delivering the candidate pages) [and our applicant tracking system, if we use one: [name]].
- Public authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context advertising.

### 5. Where your data is stored and transfers

Everything is stored in the **United States**, in AWS region us-east-1. For people in the EU or UK, this means your data is sent to and kept in a country whose law may offer less protection. We use encryption, strict access controls and short retention, and our providers are bound by data processing agreements with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum). You can ask [privacy email] for a copy.

### 6. How long we keep your data

See the **retention and destruction schedule** [link]. In short: everything that shows your face or body is destroyed no later than **90 days** after it was recorded (plus up to 14 days while encrypted backups rotate); your results are kept **1 year** [4 years for roles based in California]; the signed consent record is kept **3 years**; face measurements are never stored. We never keep biometric data more than 3 years after your last interaction with us.

### 7. Your rights

Contact [privacy email]. We answer within one month (GDPR), 45 days (California), or sooner where the law says so. We may ask you to confirm who you are.

- **Everyone:** ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it (completed within 30 days, with the limited exceptions in the retention schedule), withdraw your biometric consent at any time, and choose a video call instead of a face match.
- **EU and UK:** also object to processing based on our legitimate interests, ask us to restrict processing, receive your data in a portable form, and complain to your data protection authority ([your national authority]; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk). Where we rely on consent you may withdraw it without affecting what we did before.
- **California:** know, delete, correct, and limit the use of sensitive personal information (we use it only to provide the assessment). You will not be treated differently for using these rights. An authorised agent may act for you with your written permission. The categories we collect in the last 12 months are identifiers; audio and visual information; internet and device activity; employment-related information; and sensitive personal information (government ID image, biometric data). Their sources, purposes and retention are in the table above and the schedule. **We do not sell or share personal information.** From 1 January 2027, if California's automated-decision rules apply to us, we will add the pre-use notice and your choices to this section.
- **Illinois:** Our written biometric policy is the retention schedule [link]. We collect biometric data only after a written release (the separate tick box), we never sell it, and we destroy it as the schedule says. If an Illinois law on AI analysis of video interviews applies: we tell you here and in the consent document that software analyses your webcam picture to check presence and attention, what it looks at (where your face and eyes are, whether others are in view, whether you look at the screen), and you can refuse the face-based analysis and still be considered. On your request we delete your video within 30 days and ask anyone who received a copy to delete it too. In Illinois, we also tell you that we use AI tools in the assessment.
- **Colorado:** same written policy and consent as Illinois; the policy covers retention, deletion and a data-incident plan.
- **New York City:** if the role is based in New York City, you receive a separate notice at least 10 business days before the assessment about the characteristics assessed, how to ask for an alternative process or accommodation, and the data retention policy. [Applicability of Local Law 144 is decided in the DPIA; this line stays until counsel confirms.]
- **Everyone, if you cannot use the camera, microphone or ID check:** ask your recruiter for an adjustment.

### 8. Security and data incidents

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, keep recordings in private storage with short-lived access links, give staff only the access their role needs, and log access. We have a written procedure for data incidents. If an incident is likely to put your rights at risk, we tell the supervisory authority within 72 hours where the law requires, and we tell you when it requires that too. [Link to the short public summary of the incident response plan: needed for Colorado.]

### 9. Our staff

If you use CodeProctor as staff, we hold your name, work email, role, a password hash, optional two-factor settings, sign-in and activity logs, and the audit trail of what you opened. We use it to run the service and to show who accessed candidate data (legitimate interests). It is kept while your account is active and [3 years] after in the audit log. Contact [privacy email] for your rights.

### 10. Visitors to the candidate site

The candidate pages are delivered by Cloudflare, which sees your IP address and request details to deliver and protect the site. We use only cookies and storage that the site needs to work (for example, to keep you signed in to your session) [confirm; no analytics or advertising cookies]. Fonts and test models are served from our own site; we do not load them from third parties.

### 11. Changes and contact

We will update this notice when what we do changes, and show the date above. Privacy questions or requests: [privacy email]. Recruiter for your assessment: [recruiter name, email].

If a decision changes, so does this notice.
3. **Keep three documents in step.** Consent v0.5 (#353), retention schedule v0.6 (#354) and this notice must say the same thing about retention, withdrawal, transfers, recipients and rights. The review file lists the checks; a reviewer should diff the three before approval.
