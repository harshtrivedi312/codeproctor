Internal pilot participant consent (trial run, real data)

Version 0.1-internal-pilot · Rysun Labs Inc.

This is a short internal trial of our proctored coding assessment software, CodeProctor. You are helping us find out whether it works. Please read this page to the end. It tells you what the software records, who can see it, where it is kept, when it is deleted, and what you can choose. This trial uses your real face, voice and screen, so please read it carefully.

## 1. Taking part is voluntary

You do not have to take part. Saying no, or stopping part-way, has no effect on your job, your performance review, your pay or how anyone treats you. Nobody is told who took part except the people named in section 4. Nothing from this trial is used for any hiring, promotion or employment decision. The results of your test do not count for anything.

## 2. What the software records

When you sign below, and only after you sign, the software will:

- take a photo of your government ID and one live selfie, and compare the two faces with face-matching software;
- record your screen, your webcam video and your microphone audio while you take the test;
- record your typing in the code editor (timing, pastes and how your code changes);
- record browser events such as leaving full-screen, switching windows or pasting;
- look at your webcam picture while you work, to check that your face is in view, whether anyone else is in view, and where you are looking.

It also stores your name and work email address, your answers and code, the test score, and your IP address and browser details.

Images and measurements of your face are **biometric data**. The face-matching software calculates face measurements in the computer's memory and they are not saved. The ID photo, the selfie, the webcam video and any snapshots are saved as files.

## 3. What we use it for, and what we do not

We use it only to check that this trial of the software works: that the identity check, the recording, the playback and the deletion all behave as designed. We do not use it for any other purpose. We do not use it to train any AI model. We never sell, rent or share your biometric data, and we never use it for profit. The software does not try to read your emotions.

## 4. Who can see it

Only these people: Harsh Trivedi (the owner of this trial) and [other reviewers, if any]. They can watch the recordings and see the photos and your answers so they can check the software. They will not share what they see with anyone else, including your manager.

## 5. Where it is kept, and for how long

- Everything is stored on **one computer** owned by Rysun Labs Inc. and used only for this trial. It is **not sent to any outside company or cloud service**, and no other copy is made. We do not make backups of it.
- Everything is deleted **no later than 9 November 2026**, which is 30 days after the trial day (10 October 2026). Deleted means removed from the database and from file storage, and then confirmed by the person who ran the trial.
- If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we delete it within 7 days.
- The only thing we keep longer is this signed consent record (your typed name, the date and time, and a copy of this page), for 3 years, to show that you agreed.

This page is also our written policy for how long we keep biometric data and when we destroy it: no more than 30 days after the trial, and in any case no more than 3 years after your last contact with us.

## 6. Your choices

- You can say no now. Select I decline, and nothing is recorded.
- You can stop at any time by telling the person running the trial or by closing the test. Recording stops at once.
- You can withdraw your consent to use of your biometric data at any time, before or after the test. Tell Harsh Trivedi at [contact email]. We delete your face photos and your recordings within 7 days.
- You can ask to see what we hold about you, or to correct it.
- You can ask questions before you sign.

## 7. Contact

Questions, requests, or withdrawal: Harsh Trivedi, [contact email].
