# Internal pilot: volunteer briefing (one page)

For the Rysun staff volunteers. Hand this out or send it before the trial day. It is not the consent form; you sign that on the day, and it has the full detail.

## What this is

We are trying out our own proctored coding test, CodeProctor, on ourselves before it is used with job candidates. You will take a short coding test on [the trial computer / your own computer: coordinator to fill in] as a candidate would, and then we will check that the recording, the identity check and the deletion all worked.

## It is your choice

- **Volunteering is voluntary.** Saying no has no effect on your job, your review or your pay. Your manager will not be told who took part.
- Nothing from your test counts for anything. It is not an assessment of you.
- You can stop at any moment. You can also change your mind afterwards and ask us to delete everything within 7 days.

## What will be recorded (this is real, not practice data)

- A photo of your government ID and a live selfie, compared by face-matching software.
- Your screen, your webcam and your microphone, while you take the test.
- Your typing in the code editor, and browser events (leaving full-screen, pasting).
- Software also watches your webcam picture to see that your face is in view, that nobody else is, and where you are looking.

Please do not show anything private on your screen, and take the test alone in a quiet room, because the microphone and camera will pick up whatever is around you. If you would rather not use your own ID, say so before the day and we will discuss it.

## Who sees it, and where it lives

- Only Harsh Trivedi and [other reviewers, if any] can watch the recordings.
- It is all kept on one Rysun computer. It does not go to any outside company or cloud. We do not make backups of it.
- It is deleted no later than **9 November 2026**, and sooner if you ask. The only record kept longer is the form you sign, for 3 years, to show that you agreed.

## What we want from you

- Take the test as you normally would. Tell us anything that was confusing, broken or uncomfortable (for example, if the face check failed, or the recording felt intrusive). That is the point of the trial.
- Please do not share the test questions or links with anyone.
